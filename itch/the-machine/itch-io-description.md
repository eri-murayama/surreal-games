### English

> **An innocent middle-aged man (47) dreams of piloting a robot.**
> **Pass the compatibility test!**
> **Press, press, press — don't think!**

A 47-year-old bald man — sleeveless shirt, Pringles tube in one hand, a coat hanger in the other — has a dream.

He wants to pilot a giant robot.

Today is the day. He found the application poster in the alley.
> *"Hey, robot dreamer! You can be a part of that machine."*

He climbs into the cockpit. The pilot test begins.

### 🎮 How to play
- Click / tap to play.
- Each minigame lasts 2–4 seconds.
- Just follow the instructions.
- Don't think. Don't think. **Don't think.**

### ✨ Features
- 🤖 **3D robot battle opening** with a roaring crowd, mohawk giants, and a sea cucumber audience member
- 🎯 **28 absurd minigames** — pilot reflex tests + everyday life simulations
- 🐛 **A caterpillar to tear apart** ("Yummy!")
- 😳 **A cat-eared man to compliment** until he turns pink
- 💥 **A pasta-eared man pointing at his knee**
- 🎵 **All BGM procedurally generated** via Web Audio API + a handmade vocal song
- 🌐 **Japanese / English** (switchable in-game)
- 🎭 **3 endings** depending on how mechanical you are
- ⏱ **5–7 minutes** per playthrough

### ❗ Pilot requirements
The factory is hiring. They need someone who can:
- Operate for long durations
- Respond quickly to instructions
- Have low emotional fluctuation
- Have few relatives (preferred)

Are you the right fit?

### 🔚 Note
> *When the screen says "TEST COMPLETE", the test isn't over.*
> *Or maybe it is.*
> *You decide.*
