## Page Description (HTML/Markdown)

Yoshinori came from the countryside with one dream: to become the **Golden Gold Driver**.

With the help of a questionable Professor, he builds a racing machine via slot machine... then decides the Professor himself IS the machine.

🎰 **Spin** the slot to pick your Body, Engine, and Tires — from Sports Cars to Shopping Carts
🧑‍🔬 **Ride** the Professor (literally on all fours) into the race
🏁 **Race** against 3 rivals across 3 laps, dodging rocks and using items
🍌 **Use items** — Banana traps, Rocket boosts, and Onigiri shields

A short, absurd racing game with pixel-art style visuals, procedural chiptune BGM, and dark comedy.

**Controls:** Arrow keys or tap left/right to steer, Space or tap center to use items
**Play time:** 2-5 minutes per race
