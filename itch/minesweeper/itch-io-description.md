# Kaidan Minesweeper

A haunted twist on classic Minesweeper — with a real Japanese ghost story whispered in your ear while you play.

## Game Title
Kaidan Minesweeper

## Short Tagline
Classic Minesweeper, haunted by a Japanese ghost story.

## Description (for itch.io page)

"Kaidan" (怪談) is the Japanese word for ghost stories — the eerie, quiet, slow-burn kind told around a single candle.

Kaidan Minesweeper is the Minesweeper you know, rebuilt with a horror atmosphere: drifting purple fog, creaking floorboards, a low drone in the background, and the option to have a real Japanese ghost story murmur through your speakers while you play.

Clear the board and the spirits are laid to rest ("R.I.P..."). Hit a mine and... you are cursed.

**About the language**
- The game UI is fully in English.
- The ghost story audio is in Japanese and is left in its original language on purpose — even if you don't understand the words, the tone, pacing, and voice are the whole point of kaidan.
- You can toggle the ghost story audio on/off at any time with the "Kaidan" button.

## Features
- Classic Minesweeper rules, three difficulties (Easy / Medium / Hard)
- Atmospheric horror BGM generated in real time with the Web Audio API
- Authentic Japanese ghost story (kaidan) audio — toggleable
- Best-time records saved locally per difficulty
- Works on both desktop and mobile in a single screen (no scrolling)
- Long-press or tap a dedicated flag button to place flags on mobile

## How to Play
- **Tap / Left click**: open a cell
- **Long press / Right click**: place a flag
- **🚩 button**: toggle flag mode (so taps place flags)
- **👻 button**: reset the board
- **BGM / Kaidan buttons**: toggle the horror BGM and the ghost story audio

Clear every safe cell to send the spirits to rest. Hit a mine and you get cursed.
