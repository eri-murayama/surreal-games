# THE MACHINE - itch.io 投稿テンプレ

## ゲームタイトル
**THE MACHINE**

## サブタイトル / Tagline (60字以内)
> A 47-year-old man finally chases his dream of piloting a giant robot. ... Or does he?

## ショートディスクリプション (Short description)
> Press the button. Don't think. Become the machine.
> A surreal WarioWare-style game made for Gamedev.js Jam 2026.

---

## メイン説明文 (Description)

```
A 47-year-old bald man, sleeveless shirt, holding a Pringles tube
and a coat hanger, has a dream: to pilot a giant robot.

Today's the day. He found the application poster in the alley.
"Robot dreamer? You can be a part of that machine."

He climbs into the cockpit. The pilot test begins.

═══════════════════════════════
■ HOW TO PLAY
═══════════════════════════════

- Click / tap to play.
- Each minigame lasts 2-4 seconds.
- Just follow the instructions.
- Don't overthink it.
- Really. Don't.

═══════════════════════════════
■ FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════

▸ 28 absurd minigames (Phase 1: Robot pilot tests / Phase 2: Daily life simulation)
▸ Original illustrations of weird people (a pasta-eared man, a cat-eared uncle, a mohawk giant, a giant sea cucumber...)
▸ A handmade vocal song in the credits
▸ 3 endings depending on how mechanical you are
▸ Japanese / English support
▸ All BGM procedurally generated via Web Audio API
▸ ~5-7 minutes per playthrough

═══════════════════════════════
■ PILOT REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════

The machine factory is hiring.
They need someone who can:
 - Operate for long durations
 - Respond quickly to instructions
 - Have low emotional fluctuation
 - Have few relatives (preferred)

Are you a good fit?

═══════════════════════════════
■ CREDITS
═══════════════════════════════

Game design, illustrations, vocals: developer
Code & gameplay & BGM/SFX: AI assistant (Claude Code)

Made for Gamedev.js Jam 2026
Theme: Machines

═══════════════════════════════
■ NOTES
═══════════════════════════════

▸ Playable in browser. No install required.
▸ Audio recommended (turn it on for the full experience).
▸ Mobile-friendly (touch controls supported).
▸ Has multiple endings - try replaying.

═══════════════════════════════

When the screen says "TEST COMPLETE",
remember:
the test isn't over yet.

Or maybe it is. You decide.
```
